## Project Overview 
NAME: BREW AND BYTES 

Brew and Bytes is a modern cafe that is designed for remote workers, students, and gamers featuring high speed internet, specialty coffee and private study pods. It gives people a space to work calmly and quietly while offering coffee and tea with snacks while hard at work or just there for recreational reasons. 

The mission for my cafe is to power your productivity with world-class coffee, high-speed infrastructure, and a vibrant community where ideas come to life. Creators, coders and thinkers come to have our artisanal craft coffee where they can build connections with others. 

My future vision is for my cafe to become the premier hybrid workspace and community hub for the digital generation, setting up a new standard for how modern professionals and students collaborate, energize, and work. 

Brew and Bytes' target audiences involve students who need a quiet and productive environment to study and have a snack. Another audience is remote workers or employees who work from home but need a change of scenery for their work life and finally small tech groups to start up ideas and innovate them. Basically, at my cafe we require people who love coffee and those who love to work.

## Website Goals and Objectives
The website will be used to create an intuitive, modern digital hub that converts visitors into physical coffee shop patrons and active co-working space members and menu browsing. We will enable users to easily view workspace availability and reserve a desk or quiet pod online in under 3 clicks. Again, we will provide customers with instant access to the craft coffee and snack menu and give us a chance to showcase our products. Create and build a community and brand loyalty that supports tech students and remote workers through events and memberships tiers. 

The percentage of total website visitors who click on and interact with the menu.html page or filter categories. The average time it takes a user to select a desk, pick a time slot, and confirm a reservation which will be aimed for about 60 seconds and the membership sign up rate will be guaranteed because of the number of recurring study/work pass subscriptions generated through the site.  

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): 

Traffic: Achieve 2,500 unique monthly site visitors within four months of launch. 

Booking Inquiries: Generate at least 15 online workspaces or pod inquiries/reservations per week. 

User Engagement: Maintain an average session duration of over 2 minutes and a contact form completion rate above 5%. 

Current Website Analysis 

Strengths: 

Strong physical presence in Pretoria with direct foot traffic. 

Unique value proposition merging high-speed tech amenities with quality coffee. 

Weaknesses: 

Lack of a central digital hub for customers to view operating hours, check amenities, or pre-book workspace pods. 

Customer inquiries rely solely on walk-ins or phone calls. 

Areas for Improvement: 

Build a responsive, single/multi-page website featuring interactive contact forms, clear amenity breakdowns, staff introductions, and privacy compliance.

* **Contact & Form Cards Grid:** Styled and stacked the 4 contact info cards (`.contact-cards-grid`) and the two-column form/map layout (`.form-location-grid`) seamlessly on mobile devices.
