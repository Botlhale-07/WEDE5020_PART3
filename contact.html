Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to reserve a workspace?

Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend booking in advance during peak hours (10:00 AM - 3:00 PM) to guarantee a desk.

Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?

Yes, cancellations or rescheduling can be made up to 2 hours prior to your slot via your account dashboard or by calling us.

Do you offer day passes?

We offer flexible hourly rates, full-day passes, and monthly memberships designed to fit your schedule.
